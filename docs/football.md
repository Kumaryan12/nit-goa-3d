# Football controls and shared simulation

Join from Sports Ground or **Play football**. WASD / thumbstick moves, Shift / Run sprints, arrows / campus drag aim, Q / Pass sends a short pass, and Space / Shoot takes a shot. Running gives a stronger shot; J still jumps on desktop. Phone shot buttons live beside the movement pad, accept non-primary pointers, and suppress duplicate compatibility clicks. Help and manual kickoff are inside **Controls & kickoff**.

Football accelerates at 7 m/s² to the existing 2.5 m/s walking or 5 m/s running cap. Campus walking retains its slower acceleration. Football starts with a higher camera angle, distance 9.5 m, and a ground arrow beside the avatar so the ball and aim are readable. Drag, scroll and pinch remain available. Team admission assigns separate available spawn slots facing the correct goal, including a rotated pitch.

The authenticated server owns ball positions, goals and scores. Fixed 1/60-second physics steps consume a bounded 100 ms catch-up budget; broadcasts remain 20 Hz. Front-facing contact within 1.15 m eases the ball's velocity toward a leading position, using speed measured from accepted poses. It does not teleport the ball or capture shots over 10 m/s. One eligible dribbler per tick is chosen by distance, then stable ID. Shots are 22 m/s, running shots 30 m/s, and passes 11 m/s. Goal posts, swept goal detection, touchlines and kickoff countdowns remain shared.

Kicks carry a validated current direction. Reach is still 1.8 m; a press within 2.6 m can wait at most 180 ms for contact or cooldown. Pause, stale poses, departure, resets and expiry discard buffered presses. Rate, identity, origin, room capacity and movement validation stay on the server. Sequenced pose acknowledgements allow up to two seconds of bounded transport catch-up; the client corrects only after accepted poses stop progressing for 1.5 seconds and divergence exceeds 2 m.

The ball keeps eight snapshots on a shared timeline, renders 75 ms behind it, and predicts at most 100 ms using drag before holding position. Revision changes snap kickoff resets directly to center; goals and out-of-play positions stop prediction. A small visual blend softens packet corrections. This adds bounded display delay and does not remove latency on a poor connection. Remote players retain their existing 100 ms movement buffer and receive accepted kick animations; their per-frame state lookups use an index built on receipt. The nearest 6/8/12 visitors receive full models and labels in smooth/balanced/detailed graphics modes, with distance limits. Every other player remains visible as a single-draw-call model in the proper team colour. Detail selection updates four times a second without resetting avatar motion.

Verification includes the full regression suite, frame-rate-consistent football acceleration, jitter/reset/edge rendering, pass and shot validation, buffered-action expiry, contested touches, fresh pose acknowledgement handling, unique spawns, concurrent touch ownership and 24 authenticated loopback WebSocket peers receiving one shared pass. The development-only `/tests/fixtures/football-preview.html` rehearses the real controller, pitch and controls with in-memory room physics and optional 180 ms delayed ball snapshots. It does not connect to production or bypass authentication, and the production server does not serve it. Chrome phone emulation checks layout; a physical two-phone rehearsal remains useful for network and hardware differences.

Goals trigger a team-coloured GOAL banner and an instanced confetti burst near each player's view. The server's score and 2.5-second kickoff countdown determine the celebration identity and age: repeated packets cannot restart it, reconnects do not replay the same score, and late arrivals see only the remaining time. A local deadline clears it even if packets stop. Confetti uses one draw call, 32 pieces in smooth graphics or 64 otherwise, no shadows or collisions, and no per-frame React updates. Reduced-motion visitors receive the static score banner without moving particles or entry animation.
