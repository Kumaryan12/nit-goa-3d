# Walking arrivals

Building starts use their exterior doorway approach instead of searching outward from the footprint centre. The previous search could accept an enclosed courtyard as walkable ground, trapping visitors inside CSE/ECE's four walls.

`campusFacadeFront` supplies the same exterior face and doorway used by the rendered facade. CSE and ECE use the owner-confirmed OAT-facing side, linked by stable location IDs; their doorway, walking arrival and facade camera follow that side and later OAT location corrections. Other buildings retain their road-facing approach. Boys Hostel and Gyan Mandir retain their interior-plan entrances; Administration Block uses its existing front, with arrival beyond the porch steps. Generic buildings use an inferred road-facing exterior approach. These are the model's entrances, not a survey of every physical doorway.

The arrival search checks a small area immediately in front of the doorway, respects terrain, walls, vegetation, canal and lamp clearance, and rejects the complete outer footprint, including courtyard holes. If the approach is blocked it reports that entrance as blocked instead of silently moving the visitor to another side. Avatars face the doorway. Open-air theatre retains its entrance and faces the theatre; Main Entrance faces into campus. Explicit interior entry and football admission retain their existing destinations.

Shared arrivals use deterministic, closely spaced grid positions in front of the approach rather than scattered radial positions. Reservations keep simultaneous visitors visible; building arrivals exclude their entire footprint. Selecting another starting building prefers the doorway itself when it is unoccupied. The walking controls say **Start at**, and the information panel says **Start at entrance**.

Validation: 404 tests and the production build pass. Real published-campus geometry checks cover every named building's legal exterior arrival, facing direction, owner renaming, known interior entrances, and blocked-approach handling. Separate 32-arrival checks cover Main Entrance, CSE and ECE, including peers that have not yet published a position. Chrome also checked the actual campus controls: CSE and ECE landed 2.4 m in front of their rendered doors, Main Entrance faced into campus, and day/night views retained the exterior arrival. The full regression suite covers multiplayer, transport, collisions and interior exploration. These geometry/transport tests do not establish physical-phone performance or verify every physical campus doorway.
