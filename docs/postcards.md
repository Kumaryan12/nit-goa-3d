# Campus photo postcards

Open the camera icon beside View after campus has loaded. Drag or pinch/scroll using the existing camera controls to frame a moment, then press the shutter. The preview offers warm paper or midnight borders, a feed post (1080 × 1350, the default), story (1080 × 1920) or classic postcard (1600 × 1200), and a short caption. Story artwork leaves space at the top and bottom for social apps' controls. Download PNG works without a share service; a Share button appears when the browser supports sharing image files.

The original NIT Goa wordmark appears intact at its original aspect ratio on a white plate. It loads from the existing same-origin brand asset and is cached across exports; a failed load prevents exporting an incomplete card and can be retried. Large, wrapping captions sit over a shaded photograph, with the selected place, capture date in India, independent-project branding and OpenStreetMap attribution. Caption ideas provide quick messages; “Caption for your post” lets visitors copy a matching caption and the public campus link, with manual selection available when clipboard access fails. File sharing includes that text, though the receiving app may handle image and text differently.

The framing view temporarily hides panels and labels without changing saved view preferences. Retake returns to the camera; Exit photo mode restores normal controls. Walking and driving stop during photo mode; presence stays active and gesture animations continue, including while editing the caption. Standing walkers turn smoothly to face the camera. Seated avatars, passengers and football participants keep their existing orientation and pose.

Photo mode offers Natural, Wave, Celebrate, Heart and Dance, using the existing authenticated social-action protocol and its rate limits. Every visitor controls their own gesture. A 3-second timer is the default, with Off and 5-second alternatives. The selected gesture is refreshed near the shutter if needed, its server confirmation is checked, and a short animation-settling delay prevents capturing an unconfirmed pose. A chosen pose still has a one-second setup when the timer is Off. Cancel, leaving photo mode or hiding the tab aborts the countdown. No network protocol extension or synchronized control of other visitors is added.

“Add group names” is opt-in. At capture, the app freezes a nearby suggestion list from actual visible avatar roots, the viewer-filtered campus roster, the same floor/space, and the camera projection. It excludes avatars behind the camera, beyond 25 metres or outside the frame. Suggestions are not an occlusion detector: the photographer chooses who is actually visible in the shot. Only the photographer's name is initially selected. Up to eight displayed names can be included; changing format rechecks names against the actual crop. Names are limited to 24 characters as shown in the selector, and account IDs and handles are never exported. Turning names off also removes them from the share caption. This does not restore the removed campus location finder.

Capture renders one extra frame and immediately copies it into a bounded 2D canvas (at most 2048 pixels on its longest side). It does not enable `preserveDrawingBuffer` or read pixels continuously. PNG composition runs only for a new capture or changed postcard options. Temporary canvas memory and object URLs are released when replaced or closed. Sharing and downloads run from user gestures. Images and captions never go to the campus server or Firebase.

The photo includes the WebGL scene and avatars visible there, without interface panels, DOM name labels or embedded web/video overlays. Portrait crops the middle of the captured viewport; review the preview before saving. Export reflects the active graphics quality and cannot create detail absent from the original rendered scene.

For local review, start Vite with an empty `VITE_FIREBASE_API_KEY` and open `/tests/fixtures/campus-ui-preview.html`. This development-only fixture renders the real campus without changing production admission or the maintenance deadline. `/tests/fixtures/photo-group-preview.html` provides three synthetic visitors using the production avatar, capture and postcard components. It proves visual pose/name composition without claiming a real hosted multiplayer rehearsal. Review all formats and both themes, caption presets/copy, timers/cancel, retake, close/Escape, repeated captures, portrait phone sizing, and a downloaded PNG. Unit coverage checks camera projection and group filtering, crop/name changes, story margins, social caption text, text limits and canvas allocation errors. Existing social-server tests cover shared actions, admission and rate limits.
