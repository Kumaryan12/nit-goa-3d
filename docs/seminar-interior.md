# Seminar Complex courtyard and halls

Implemented locally on 14 October 2026, using the owner-corrected identity `way/1423803680`. The owner described a courtyard immediately after entering, a hall with seats on the left and Sal Seminar Hall without seats on the right. The seated hall is Chapora Hall. The owner also confirmed left and right stair flights joining a common upper landing behind both halls, lower access from the courtyard, and descending floors in both halls. The owner clarified that Sal descends in steps, while Chapora has a continuous slope. Left and right are relative to entering the courtyard from its western opening, facing east.

## Walking and multiplayer

The normal swept indoor movement blocks walls, seats, stages, planters and rails and preserves door clearance. It follows the same stepped and sloped elevation profile used by the renderer. Small swept steps permit ordinary walking up/down both entrance flights, Sal’s terraces and Chapora’s slope. The larger step-up allowance is restricted to Sal, preventing a teleport between the raised landing and ground passage. Height-aware walls, landing headroom and camera checks distinguish both layers. The courtyard has open-sky jumping, while hall ceilings and door headers limit jumping. `seminar:0` is the only accepted new multiplayer space; nearby chat and avatars stay separate from outdoors, the hostel and Gyan Mandir.
