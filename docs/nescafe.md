The 4.2 m height and furnishings are visual estimates, not a surveyed reconstruction; the mapped dimensions are retained, with an owner-requested 90° anticlockwise rotation about the building centre. Foundations, service windows, furniture colliders and seat heights follow that orientation.

The road lies immediately beside the front façade. The original sports-ground-facing terrace now faces west after rotating the whole café, with four round tables, 16 green-and-cream chairs, timber pergola slats, canvas shade ribbons, potted planting, coffee cups and warm festoon lights. A clear central aisle and perimeter routes leave every chair approachable. Trees, garden beds and meadow generation reserve the terrace. Paving follows the existing terrain instead of flattening or changing it.

Disconnects and existing activity timeouts release seats. ## Coffee breaks

A compact **What’s new on campus** card waits until the loading intro finishes and campus admission succeeds. It previews unread entries from the shared update feed, rather than advertising only the café. **See all updates** opens a scrollable panel inside campus with new experiences visitors can try, plus shortcuts to Nescafé, football and OAT. Maintenance notes and visual or performance improvements stay in the GitHub changelog. **What’s new** in campus tools reopens this panel, and `/updates` uses the same feed.

Acknowledgment saves the current entry IDs in this browser, so ordinary reloads do not repeat them. Add each future visitor-facing feature to `campusUpdates` in `src/data/campusUpdate.ts` with a new, unique ID; it automatically becomes unread for returning visitors. Automatic notices wait while other activity panels are in use. No profile records, notification permissions or extra server requests are involved. Storage-disabled visitors can still dismiss the card for their current visit.

Walk up to either vending window in avatar mode. The compact **At Nescafé** menu opens when you stop at the counter, once per visit, without stealing movement focus. Choose **Coffee**, **Cold coffee** or **Water**; the selected drink is highlighted. Closing the menu keeps it closed during that visit. Leaving counter range for at least three seconds allows a new arrival prompt; walking, riding or another active activity never triggers the prompt.

The avatar carries one drink for five minutes. **Sip coffee**, **Sip cold coffee** or **Drink water** animates the right arm without changing walking, seat position or collision physics. **Put away** removes the drink immediately. The same menu offers **Sit at a table** beside an unoccupied café chair, or **Stand up** while seated. Seating still uses the server's proximity and exclusive-seat checks. Café seats continue to work while carrying a drink, and movement remains available after using the menu. Drinks clear when joining football/concerts, entering an interior or mounting a vehicle. Reduced-motion visitors hold a still drink pose.

Drinks are virtual, free and temporary. They do not make purchases, modify profiles or affect movement speed. The existing authenticated campus socket handles orders and shared state. The server checks the accepted active outdoor pose, freshness, stationary on-foot status, counter distance and height, and outward-side clearance. It chooses timestamps, restricts drink kinds, limits requests to one per three seconds and ignores client identities, coordinates and expiry timestamps. Other visitors see the same held drink and sip time; older clients safely ignore the added metadata. The server publishes five counter positions from the same saved campus model during the build. Both frontend and backend must be deployed for live ordering.

Each detailed avatar's held drink uses one small merged mesh with no texture download, transparency, particles or additional shadow light. Nearby avatars retain their articulated drink pose; distant simplified avatars omit that detail. Pose updates stay in refs; React updates only when drink selection or sip state changes. The mobile menu uses the existing translucent popover and touch-size buttons.

## Live table conversations — 13 October 2026

Sit at any of the four tables and open **Nescafé → Table conversation**. **Start talking** asks for microphone permission; **Listen** joins with the mic off. Callers can mute their mic, mute individual tablemates, adjust listening volume, leave voice, order any of the three drinks and sip while seated. Drinks are available from every accepted café chair, including chairs beyond counter range. A small microphone pill remains accessible when campus tools are hidden. Closing the café menu preserves the call. Standing via café controls, Actions or movement first asks **“Do you want to leave the conversation?”**. **Stay seated** preserves the seat and voice; **Leave and stand up** disconnects voice before requesting standing. **Leave voice** also asks before ending an established conversation. Forced disconnects, tab hiding and quota shutdown still stop capture immediately. Leaving an active conversation shows “You left the table conversation.” for five seconds, including when standing through the café menu, Actions or movement; it stays visible with the toolbar hidden. Standing, changing activities, hiding the page or losing the connection stops capture and closes its audio graph.

Voice signalling shares the authenticated presence socket. The server derives table membership from exclusive, published seats and a fresh accepted outdoor walking pose; clients cannot supply a table or someone else's identity. Only opted-in callers at the same table receive signalling or short-lived relay configuration. Fresh join IDs isolate late offers, answers and ICE after changing seats, disconnecting or rejoining. Each table has at most four callers and each browser at most three audio peer links. Signals and mic changes are bounded; video and data-channel offers are rejected. Audio never runs through the avatar animation or the 10 Hz presence snapshot loop. Ordered initiators avoid simultaneous-offer conflicts; early ICE is bounded and queued, and connection retries are limited.

The OAT and café share one relay provider and the existing Cloudflare usage monitor, warning thresholds and 900 GB early cutoff. No new provider, billing plan, credentials, public Firebase reads or recording/storage of audio was added. Table participants see the same aggregate usage. Usage becoming stale, unavailable or over cutoff stops calls. Production must deploy **both backend and frontend** for this feature; an older backend cannot acknowledge table voice.
