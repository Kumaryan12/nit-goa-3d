# Pinch zoom and immersive view

Both explorer modes keep ordinary wheel/two-finger scroll zoom and add explicit trackpad pinch handling. Walk mode now tracks two touch contacts: spreading fingers reduces follow distance, closing them increases it, and camera rotation stops while pinching. Lifting one finger resumes dragging from its last position. Third contacts suspend changes; cancellation, capture loss, blur and unmount clear held contacts. The existing 3–12 metre follow range and smoothing remain.

Overview's camera-controls uses two-touch dolly rather than combined dolly/pan. Chromium/Firefox-style Ctrl-wheel pinch and Safari gesture events change bounded camera distance. Capture-phase interception prevents a trackpad pinch being handled twice or zooming the browser page. Native touchscreen dolly remains owned by camera-controls; simultaneous Safari touchscreen gesture events are ignored to avoid doubling it. Pinch is scoped to the scene canvas; browser zoom outside the scene remains available. Camera position persists across resizing and fullscreen changes.

The bottom-right View dock remains reachable with the toolbar hidden. Checkboxes control exploration side panels, minimap and toolbar independently. Focus view hides all three; Show controls restores the preceding layout. Local storage saves the current layout. Walking movement, jump/run/pause and doorway/stair actions stay in a compact panel. Selecting or inspecting a place reveals its details. Explicitly opened chat, concert and editing tools retain their own close controls rather than silently hiding an active microphone or editing operation.

Fullscreen uses the browser Fullscreen API from a button click, synchronizes its exit state through fullscreenchange, and offers Focus view when unsupported. Loading failures/retry controls remain visible in Focus view. Attribution remains visible in Overview. Mobile spacing reserves room for the dock, and a visible walking minimap keeps its compact top position.

Validation: 386 tests pass; TypeScript and production build pass. Five new gesture tests cover spread/close direction, pinch-to-drag transitions, third-contact/cancel/blur/unmount recovery, ordinary scroll versus Ctrl-wheel, Safari deduplication, invalid values and cumulative zoom. Chrome Guest browser checks verified actual fullscreen entry/exit state, Focus view in Overview and night Walk mode, compact walking controls, and independent minimap/panel/toolbar restoration. Physical two-finger touchscreen and Safari trackpad hardware were not available for this verification; their event paths are tested with simulated contacts/gestures. A mobile hardware pass remains useful for sensitivity and browser-specific fullscreen limits.
